Baseline minus extended RMSE: −0.00122. The paired bootstrap 95% interval is [−0.00633, 0.00377]. This run does not establish a benefit from nominal formations. The individual model is also practically tied with persistence.

Scope and known limitations

The target is first-season attacking output. The cohort excludes players with fewer than 450 destination-season minutes; failed transfers may be underrepresented. Position is taken from a later profile snapshot. Formation history is a weak tactical proxy; pressing and possession are absent. Changing destination context is not a causal estimate, and its shortlist accuracy has not been validated by scouts.
